# Statewide Rollout Strategy & Phased Roadmap

## 1. Phased Rollout Cadence
```
[ Phase A: Controlled Technical Pilot (Month 1) ]
  - Revenue & Social Welfare Departments (Chennai & Salem)
  - 50 e-Sevai centers; internal supervision
         │
         ▼
[ Phase B: Limited Department Pilot (Month 2) ]
  - Expand to School Education, Health, and Agriculture
  - Onboard 150 additional e-Sevai kiosks across 5 districts
         │
         ▼
[ Phase C: Multi-District Expansion (Month 3-4) ]
  - All 38 District Collectorates live on Contact Directory
  - Automated transfer sync with Public (Special.A) Dept
         │
         ▼
[ Phase D: Statewide Information Portal (Month 5-6) ]
  - All 45 Secretariat Departments live
  - Public statewide launch of Portal 2.0 with AI Citizen Assistant
         │
         ▼
[ Phase E: Advanced Transactional Integrations (Month 7+) ]
  - Direct end-to-end payment gateway and digilocker locker integration
```
