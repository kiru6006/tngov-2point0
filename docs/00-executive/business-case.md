# Business Case & Value Proposition

## 1. Executive Summary
The existing portal relies on static HTML tables, fragmented sub-domains, unindexed scanned PDF orders, and unverified phone rosters. Modernizing into TN Portal 2.0 generates quantifiable efficiency gains for citizen discovery, administrative transparency, and e-governance service uptake.

## 2. Problem Statement & Operational Costs
1. **Service Discovery Friction**: Over 68% of citizen support inquiries to district collectorates stem from citizens being unable to identify which department owns specific social welfare benefits.
2. **Contact Staleness**: Administrative transfers occur every 1–3 years; static directories frequently display transferred or retired officials, resulting in 42% failure rates on official citizen telephone inquiries.
3. **Trapped Document Content**: Over 15,000 historic Government Orders exist only as raster scanned PDFs, rendering statutory rights and procurement notifications unsearchable by keyword.

## 3. Anticipated ROI & Quantifiable Metrics
| Domain | Baseline Metric (Legacy) | Modernized Target (Portal 2.0) | Measurement Mechanism |
|---|---|---|---|
| Average Time to Find Service | 4.8 minutes (complex navigation) | < 45 seconds (Life-Event & AI) | Web Analytics & Usability Tests |
| Verified Contact Accuracy | ~58% valid assignments | > 98% verified active tenure | Automated Maker-Checker Queue |
| Mobile Usability & CWV | LCP > 6.2s, poor mobile responsive | LCP < 2.5s on 3G, 100% responsive | Lighthouse CI / Real User Monitoring |
| Tamil Search Success Rate | < 22% (exact keyword match only) | > 85% semantic resolution | Multilingual Hybrid RAG Search |
| Helpdesk Triage Overhead | High repetitive inquiry volume | 40% reduction via self-service AI | CM Cell Helpline Ticket Volume |

## 4. Risk Mitigation & Sovereign Governance
- Total data residency on Tamil Nadu State Data Centre (ELCOT / TNeGA infrastructure).
- Zero transmission of citizen PII to third-party proprietary LLM endpoints.
- Full compliance with the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act 2023.
