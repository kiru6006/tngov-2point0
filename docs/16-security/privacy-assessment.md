# Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Privacy Assessment

## 1. Data Minimization Principles
- Contact directory exposes strictly official coordinates: designation, office phone, official email, and room address.
- Citizens querying services and schemes are not required to provide Aadhaar numbers, PAN, or biometric information during discovery.
- Application tracking requires only the service application reference number.

## 2. Retention & Anonymization Policy
- Citizen AI chat queries are retained for 30 days for service quality improvement and audit compliance.
- Queries are automatically scrubbed of phone numbers, Aadhaar patterns, and email addresses prior to storage.
