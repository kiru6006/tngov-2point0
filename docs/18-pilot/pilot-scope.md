# Pilot Scope & Phased Rollout Strategy

## 1. Pilot Rollout Phases
- **Phase 1: Controlled Internal Pilot (Week 1-2)**:
  - Scope: TNeGA administrators and Revenue Department nodal officers (Salem & Chennai).
  - Target: Master directory verification, transfer workflow testing, and OCR accuracy validation.
- **Phase 2: Assisted Access Kiosk Pilot (Week 3-4)**:
  - Scope: 50 selected e-Sevai centers across urban and rural taluks.
  - Target: Operator-assisted welfare applications and application status tracking.
- **Phase 3: Public Beta (Week 5-6)**:
  - Scope: Public access with AI Citizen Assistant enabled for top 10 high-volume services.
- **Phase 4: Full Statewide Launch (Week 7+)**:
  - Scope: All 45 departments and 38 districts live.
