# Bilingual Localization Standard (English & Tamil)
**Document Reference: TN-GOV-2.0-MOD-06**

---

### 1. English as Default Language
Per P0 specifications, the portal initializes in English (`en`). All initial state hooks default to `"en"`, with instantaneous reactive toggling to Tamil (`"ta"`).

### 2. Tamil Language Quality & Typography
All Tamil strings are verified against official Government of Tamil Nadu terminology and comply with Unicode block `஀-௿`.
