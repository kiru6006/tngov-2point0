# Department-Wise Performance Reporting
**Document Reference: TN-GOV-2.0-MOD-12**

---

### 1. Mandatory Core KPIs
Performance reporting at `/performance` computes 6 key indicators:
1. **Total Received**: Cumulative petitions and service applications registered.
2. **Total Resolved**: Verified completed disposals with Action Taken Reports.
3. **Total Pending**: Active cases progressing within statutory SLA windows.
4. **Total Overdue**: Cases breaching statutory deadlines.
5. **Median Resolution Time**: Statewide median business days from registration to disposal.
6. **SLA Compliance %**: Percentage of total disposals completed within statutory charter timeframes.
