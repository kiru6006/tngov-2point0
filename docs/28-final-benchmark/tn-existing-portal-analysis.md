# Tamil Nadu Existing Portal (tn.gov.in) Baseline Analysis

## 1. Source & Legacy Overview
- **Source URL**: `https://www.tn.gov.in/`
- **Current Architecture**: Traditional server-rendered PHP / static HTML structure with separate sub-domains for departments, directories, gazettes, and tenders.

---

## 2. Existing Deficiencies & Usability Gaps
1. **Search Over-Reliance**: Citizens cannot easily browse services without knowing exact administrative terms.
2. **Deep Navigational Hops**: Finding a certificate or G.O. requires navigating 3 to 5 levels deep across disparate sub-domains.
3. **Buried Contact Information**: Contact directory is isolated in PDF tables or secondary pages (`contact_directory.php`, `minister_list.php`).
4. **Lack of Inline Service Action**: Service cards only link to generic external portals without displaying eligibility, SLA, or fee checklists.
5. **Mobile Responsiveness**: Tables and forms cause horizontal scrolling and layout breaking on small viewports.

---

## 3. Preserved Capabilities in TN 2.0
- Official department taxonomy (45 departments).
- 38 District Collectorates and administrative headquarters.
- Verified statutory citizen charters and fee structures.
- Direct statutory e-Sevai, Patta/Chitta, and TNPDS integration points.
