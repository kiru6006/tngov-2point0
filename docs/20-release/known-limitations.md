# Known Technical Limitations & Operational Boundaries

1. **Legacy Scanned Tamil Documents**: Very old physical G.O. documents (pre-2005) with faded typewritten Tamil scripts require human review queue escalation due to optical noise in OCR.
2. **Third-Party External Portal Availability**: External department payment gateways or legacy land servers (`eservices.tn.gov.in`) may experience external maintenance windows independent of Portal 2.0 availability.
3. **Non-Synchronous Off-Line Gazette Issuance**: When an officer is transferred by internal government order before gazette publication, the system depends on the Department Nodal Officer to submit a change request within 24 hours of notification.
