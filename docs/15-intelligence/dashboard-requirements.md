# Administrative Intelligence Dashboard Requirements

## 1. Objective
Empower department heads, district collectors, and TNeGA administrators with real-time observability over citizen inquiries, search trends, data quality anomalies, and AI accuracy metrics.

## 2. Key Dashboard Modules
1. **AI Performance & Quality Overview**:
   - Total queries processed, grounded accuracy rate, citizen satisfaction index, citation coverage.
2. **Citizen Inquiries & Search Trend Analytics**:
   - Top queried services (e.g., Patta Chitta, Community Certificate), emerging demand patterns, zero-result search queries.
3. **Directory Completeness & Freshness**:
   - Positions vacant, contacts missing phone/email, stale records awaiting verification.
4. **Autonomous AI Review Queue**:
   - Flags ungrounded answers, negative citizen ratings, and suspected prompt injections for human nodal officer review.
