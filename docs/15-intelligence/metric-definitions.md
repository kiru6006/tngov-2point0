# Metric Definitions & Administrative Telemetry Formulas

## 1. Quality & Accuracy Metrics
- **Grounded Answer Rate (%)**:
  $$\frac{\text{Queries answered with 100\% verified citations}}{\text{Total inquiries}} \times 100$$
- **Hallucination Rate (%)**:
  $$\frac{\text{Answers flagged with factual deviations in review queue}}{\text{Total inquiries}} \times 100$$
- **Citizen Satisfaction Score (%)**:
  $$\frac{\text{Positive Feedback (Helpful)}}{\text{Total Feedback Submissions}} \times 100$$

## 2. Directory Health Metrics
- **Directory Completeness Index (%)**:
  $$\frac{\text{Positions with verified active official, contact, and email}}{\text{Total sanctioned positions}} \times 100$$
- **Stale Record Ratio (%)**:
  $$\frac{\text{Records unverified for } > 90 \text{ days}}{\text{Total records}} \times 100$$
