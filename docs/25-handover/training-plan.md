# Government Operational Training & Enablement Plan

## 1. Role-Specific Training Modules
- **Module 1: Citizen-First Portal Navigation** (For e-Sevai Kiosk Operators, 2 Hours).
  - Driving the Assisted Access interface, verifying applicant documents, printing statutory receipts.
- **Module 2: Directory Master Data Governance** (For Department Nodal Editors, 3 Hours).
  - Creating change requests, attaching gazette evidence, proposing officer transfers.
- **Module 3: Verification & Checker Oversight** (For Department Checkers / Joint Secretaries, 2 Hours).
  - Maker-checker verification, audit trail inspection, preventing duplicate cadre postings.
- **Module 4: SRE & Incident Command** (For TNeGA Technical Staff, 4 Hours).
  - Telemetry monitoring, alert triage, AI feature flag killswitch operation, disaster recovery execution.
