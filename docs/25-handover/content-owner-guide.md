# Department Content Owner Guide

## 1. Content Verification Standards
1. **Statutory Services**: Must provide parallel Tamil and English text, statutory SLA days under Citizen Charter, fee structure, and official e-Sevai link.
2. **Flagship Schemes**: Must extract eligibility rules verbatim from approved government orders. Numeric ceilings (e.g. income thresholds) must cite issuing department G.O.
3. **Government Orders**: Must be uploaded with machine-readable text and cryptographically verified PDF download URLs. Superseded orders must be marked `SUPERSEDED` immediately upon revocation.
