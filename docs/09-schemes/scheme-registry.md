# Government Schemes & Welfare Benefits Registry

## 1. Grounded Official Welfare Schemes
Tamil Nadu flagship welfare schemes are indexed with explicit statutory eligibility rules:

| Scheme ID | Scheme Name (English / Tamil) | Administering Department | Beneficiaries & Key Benefit |
|---|---|---|---|
| `SCH-001` | Kalaignar Magalir Urimai Thittam (KMUT) / கலைஞர் மகளிர் உரிமைத் திட்டம் | Social Welfare & Women Empowerment | Eligible women heads of households: ₹1,000 monthly DBT |
| `SCH-002` | Pudhumai Penn / புதுமைப் பெண் திட்டம் | Higher Education Department | Govt school female students pursuing college: ₹1,000 monthly |
| `SCH-003` | Chief Minister's Breakfast Scheme / முதலமைச்சரின் காலை உணவுத் திட்டம் | Social Welfare / Municipal Admin | Primary school children (Classes 1-5): Hot cooked breakfast |
| `SCH-004` | Makkalai Thedi Maruthuvam / மக்களைத் தேடி மருத்துவம் | Health & Family Welfare | Senior citizens / NCD patients: Home delivery of essential medicines |
| `SCH-005` | Innuyir Kaappom (Nammai Kaakkum 48) / இன்னுயிர் காப்போம் | Health & Family Welfare | Road accident victims: Free emergency care up to ₹1 Lakh in 48 hrs |
| `SCH-006` | Naan Mudhalvan / நான் முதல்வன் | Special Programme Implementation | College youth: Free industry technical training & placement |
| `SCH-007` | Kuruvai Cultivation Special Farmers Package | Agriculture & Farmers Welfare | Delta paddy farmers: Certified seed & fertilizer subsidies |
| `SCH-008` | Old Age Pension (OAP) / முதியோர் ஓய்வூதியத் திட்டம் | Revenue & Disaster Management | Destitute senior citizens (60+): ₹1,200 monthly pension |

## 2. Zero-Hallucination Policy
- AI assistant cannot grant or adjudicate welfare eligibility.
- Official eligibility criteria must cite authoritative G.O. guidelines.
