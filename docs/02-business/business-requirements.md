# Business Requirements Document (BRD v1.0)

## 1. Scope & Objectives
1. Eliminate citizen navigation friction: Replace departmental silos with intent-driven Life Event taxonomies.
2. Unified Service Transparency: Display statutory SLAs, fees, and required documents before external portal redirects.
3. Universal Search: Sub-second bilingual semantic search resolving conversational citizen queries.

## 2. Non-Negotiable Business Rules
- **Rule 01**: No live government service, contact, or document removed without signed verification.
- **Rule 02**: No fabricated official names, phone numbers, or designations. Source URL and audit timestamp mandatory.
- **Rule 03**: Tamil and English maintained with parity across all navigation, metadata, and search indexing.
- **Rule 04**: No PII retained on public portal (DPDP Act compliance). Transactional payloads routed directly to TNeGA/department gateways.
