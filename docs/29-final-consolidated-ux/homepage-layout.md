# Final Homepage Layout: 17 Ordered Sections

The homepage follows a strict progressive disclosure sequence designed to minimize interaction friction:

1. **Government Utility Header**: Language toggle (English/தமிழ்), GIGW Font Sizer (A-, A, A+), High Contrast (7:1), Skip link (`#main-content`), e-Sevai Kiosk mode, Help (`#emergency-help`), Sitemap (`#sitemap`).
2. **Government Identity & Navigation**: Official Emblem, bilingual government titles, universal search bar, "All Services" button, mobile drawer toggle.
3. **Citizen Search Hero**: "All Government Services. One Place." / "அனைத்து அரசு சேவைகளும். ஒரே இடத்தில்." + Quick search tag suggestions + Grounded zero-generation AI result card.
4. **Popular Services (Quick Access Services)**: 12 high-priority visible service cards with statutory SLAs, fee structures, and application links.
5. **Browse All Services by Category**: Grid of 14 service domains with direct deep links.
6. **Service Action Panel (Inline Discovery - AP Pattern)**: Interactive tab selector that immediately reveals constituent services, SLAs, and direct "Apply" / "Track" action buttons inline without navigating away.
7. **Browse by Life Situation**: Targeted entitlement tabs: Students, Farmers, Women, Senior Citizens, Entrepreneurs, Job Seekers.
8. **Government Contact Directory**: Real-time search by Name, Designation, Department, District, and quick links for Ministers, Secretaries, Collectors, RTI.
9. **Government and Administration**: Clean constitutional separation of Executive, Legislature, and Judiciary.
10. **Departments and Districts**: 38-District interactive explorer with instant Collectorate phone, email, and portal links + 45 departments link.
11. **Government Orders & Documents**: 6-tab notice board (GOs, Notifications, Circulars, Tenders, Recruitment, Announcements) with dates and download links.
12. **e-Governance Portal Directory**: Curated directory of 6 verified state platforms (e-Sevai, Patta, TNPDS, Guidance, CCTNS, CMDA).
13. **Schemes and Benefits**: Flagship welfare initiatives with eligibility, benefit amounts, and application modes.
14. **Announcements & Recruitment**: Latest official notifications from DIPR, TNPSC, and TRB.
15. **Tourism and Culture**: Curated heritage showcase featuring UNESCO Living Chola Temples, Mamallapuram, Nilgiris Mountain Railway, and TTDC links.
16. **Emergency & Grievance Help**: 8 24x7 crisis response helplines (CM 1100, 108, 100, 101, 181, 1098, 1077, 1930).
17. **Comprehensive Footer**: Categorized 6-column sitemap + GIGW 3.0 / WCAG 2.2 AA legal policies and accessibility disclaimer.
