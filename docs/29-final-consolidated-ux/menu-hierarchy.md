## 2. Citizen Services Mega Menu (14 Verified Categories)
1. **Certificates**: Income, Community, Nativity, Birth, Death, Legal Heir
2. **Land and Property**: Patta/Chitta, Encumbrance Certificate, Registration, TSLR
3. **Education**: Scholarships, Student Services, EMIS, Examinations
4. **Employment**: Job Exchange Registration, Skill Training, TNPSC
5. **Health**: CM Comprehensive Health Insurance, Hospital Services, Certificates
6. **Agriculture**: Uzhavan App, PM-KISAN, Free Power, Micro-Irrigation
7. **Electricity and Utilities**: TANGEDCO Bill, Metro Water, Property Tax
8. **Transport**: Driving Licence, Vehicle Registration, TNSTC Bus
9. **Housing**: Tamil Nadu Housing Board (TNHB), TN-RERA Sanctions
10. **Business and Industry**: Guidance Single Window, MSME, Licences
11. **Social Welfare**: Kalaignar Magalir Urimai, Old Age Pension, Disability
12. **Public Safety**: Police CCTNS, Kavalan SOS, Fire NOC
13. **Grievances**: CM Helpline 1100, Mudhalvar Mugavari, RTI
14. **Local Body Services**: Urban Civic Payments, Birth/Death extracts, Trade Permits
