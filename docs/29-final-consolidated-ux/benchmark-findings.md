# Consolidated Benchmark Findings: AP + Telangana + UP for Tamil Nadu 2.0

## 1. Executive Summary
This document synthesizes the best-of-breed design patterns across three premier Indian state government portals to create the definitive Tamil Nadu Government Digital Portal 2.0:
1. **Andhra Pradesh (`ap.gov.in`)**: Transformed passive card displays into an **Inline Service Action Panel** that immediately reveals actionable citizen services with SLAs, fees, application links, and tracking lookups.
2. **Telangana (`telangana.gov.in`)**: Provided institutional rigor by cleanly separating the three constitutional branches (Executive, Legislature, Judiciary) and establishing an exhaustive Government Orders (G.O.) architecture.
3. **Uttar Pradesh (`up.gov.in`)**: Introduced citizen-centric categorization by life events (Students, Farmers, Women, Seniors, Entrepreneurs) and a high-utility 24x7 emergency helpline matrix.

---

## 2. Synthesis Matrix for Tamil Nadu 2.0
- **Navigation**: 8-item primary menu with accessible full-width mega menus.
- **Service Discovery**: 14 distinct statutory service categories, backed by inline instant service reveal.
- **Service Details**: Uniform 18-attribute specification page with clear checklists, G.O. statutory citations, and direct TNeGA e-Sevai / department links.
- **Administration**: Separate Executive, Legislative, and Judicial portals integrated under `/departments` and `/contact-directory`.
- **District Federation**: 38 Tamil Nadu districts accessible through interactive selector with direct Collectorate contact details.
