# Controlled Pilot Scope & Boundaries

## 1. Pilot Focus & Boundaries
The controlled pilot evaluates core citizen discovery and administrative directory management across a targeted footprint:
- **Participating Departments**:
  1. Revenue & Disaster Management Department
  2. Social Welfare & Women Empowerment Department
  3. School Education Department
  4. Health & Family Welfare Department
- **Participating Districts**:
  1. Chennai District (Urban administration)
  2. Salem District (Rural/Semi-urban administration)
- **Included Services**: High-volume certificates (Community, Income, Nativity), land records (Patta Chitta), and welfare schemes (Kalaignar Magalir Urimai Thogai).
- **Public Restriction**: Pilot environment accessible exclusively via secured ingress tokens and designated e-Sevai kiosk static IP ranges.
