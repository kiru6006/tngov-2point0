# Government Data Verification & Authority Audit

## 1. Verified Official Datasets
All data within the portal release candidate was audited against official government sources:
1. **Ministers & Leadership**: Verified against Gazette Extraordinary Notification No. 1 and official CM portal records.
2. **Secretaries & District Collectors**: Verified against Public (Special.A) Department postings and District Administration portals.
3. **Statutory Services**: Seeded against Revenue & Disaster Management Department and TNeGA e-Sevai published citizen charters.
4. **Welfare Schemes**: Seeded against Social Welfare & Women Empowerment statutory program guidelines.
5. **Government Orders**: Sourced with verifiable PDF download URLs and cryptographic SHA-256 integrity hashes.
